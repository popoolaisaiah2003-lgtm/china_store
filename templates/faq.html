Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding peptide purity, COA verification, cold-chain shipping, and WhatsApp order fulfillment.

What is the purity standard of AntiGravity Peptides?

All our peptides are analytical grade with guaranteed sequence purity ≥ 99.0% (many ≥ 99.8%). Every batch is tested via High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) and Mass Spectrometry (MS).

How does WhatsApp Ordering work?

On any product page, choose your quantity and destination country, then click "Send Order Inquiry via WhatsApp". This automatically formats a message sent directly to our official line (+234 818 188 2418) where our desk will process payment and shipping options.

How are orders shipped to preserve peptide integrity?

Peptides are shipped in durable lyophilized vial format inside climate-insulated thermal packaging. Lyophilized peptides remain stable at ambient temperatures during typical transit times.

Are certificates of analysis (COA) provided?

Yes! A Certificate of Analysis detailing purity levels, mass spec verification, and lot numbers is available upon request for every lot number.
